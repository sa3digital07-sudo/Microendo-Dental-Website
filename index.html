Our treatments

Dental care for your next step.

Explore common dental care options. Your dentist can assess your needs and explain which treatments may be suitable for you.

✦

Root Canal Treatment

Assessment and treatment options for teeth with inflamed or infected pulp.

What to expect

A dentist examines the tooth, usually with an X-ray, then removes the inflamed or infected pulp, cleans the canals and seals the tooth. Ask the clinic how many visits your tooth may need.

⌕

Micro-Endodontics

Specialist endodontic techniques using magnification where appropriate.

What to expect

A dental microscope gives a magnified view of tiny canals. It can help in selected cases, such as retreatment or complex canal anatomy.

Enquire about treatment ↗
✚

Conservative Dentistry

Care focused on preserving natural tooth structure whenever possible.

What to expect

The aim is to repair decay or damage while keeping as much healthy tooth as possible. Your dentist will explain the options for your tooth.

☼

Laser Dentistry

Ask the clinic about available laser-assisted dental procedures.

What to expect

Lasers may be used for some soft-tissue and other procedures. Call the clinic to confirm which laser procedures are available.

Dental Fillings

Consultation for tooth decay and suitable restorative options.

What to expect

After removing decay, the dentist restores the tooth with a filling material. Ask which material suits your tooth and why.

General Consultation

Discuss tooth pain, oral health concerns and preventive dental care.

What to expect

Come in for tooth pain, sensitivity, a check-up or a second opinion. The dentist will examine you and explain what they find.
